F1viz · 2026 Season Film

Design & Build

Designed and built by Carlo De Marchis — “A guy with a scarf”. The concept, the ranking-ordered card-film layout, the visual language and the interactions — the season films, the per-driver position traces, the self-playing lap-evolution timing tower — are my own. An alternative, more playful way to move through a Formula 1 season, told in my own style.

Data & Images

Every number and image here comes from official and open sources, and belongs entirely to them. Results, classifications, pit-stop data, circuit maps, driver & car imagery and race-highlights links are from Formula 1 (formula1.com) — property of the Formula One group. Lap-by-lap timing, positions and lap times come from jolpica-f1 (the community Ergast successor); safety-car & VSC data from OpenF1 (openf1.org). All of it is used here only to power this fan experience — the credit belongs to the rights-holders.

Built With

Designed and developed with Claude (Anthropic) — Claude Code for the build. A single self-contained page in vanilla JavaScript, hosted on GitHub Pages, with privacy-friendly Cloudflare Web Analytics.
